Writing Constraints

Frameworks to ground your orientation-first drafting process.

Reader State Declaration

A Reader State Declaration (30 seconds, optional but powerful)
Before drafting, answer one line:
The reader is currently feeling ___ and is stuck between ___ and ___.

Why this matters:
● Prevents accidental preaching
● Prevents writing “from above”
● Keeps tone grounded, not instructive

The Stop Rule

A Stop Rule (to prevent over-writing)
Your system is orientation-first, which creates a risk: continuing to write past clarity.

Explicit stop condition:
I stop writing when the confusion I named at the start has been dissolved—even if the piece feels short.

This prevents:
● depth theater
● unnecessary elaboration
● “one more insight” syndrome
