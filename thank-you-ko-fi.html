Ko-fi Support Email Toolkit

Thank your supporters and guide them on how to stay connected.

Subject

Thank you for your support

Body

Hi [Name],

Thank you for supporting my work on Ko-fi. I genuinely appreciate it.

I use Ko-fi as a quiet members-only space where I write when there’s something worth writing—no fixed schedule, no pressure.

One small practical note: Ko-fi only sends notifications for new posts if notifications are enabled on your account.

If you’d like to be notified when I publish, you can enable this by:
● Logging in to Ko-fi
● Going to Settings → Notifications
● Turning on email or platform notifications for creators you support

This is entirely optional—just sharing it so you’re aware.

Either way, thank you for being here.
— Siva
